## Baobab: Africa's Timeless Superfruit

Baobab (Adansonia digitata) is often called the **"Tree of Life"** — and for good reason. For centuries, communities across Africa have consumed its pulp for strength, immunity, and skin resilience.

> “Baobab powder contains up to 10x more vitamin C than oranges.” — [BBC Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/howto/guide/health-benefits-baobab)

This high vitamin C content makes it especially powerful for **collagen production** — keeping your skin firm, plump, and youthful.

---

## Why Baobab Supports Healthy Skin

### 🌟 Rich in Antioxidants

Baobab fights oxidative stress — a major cause of premature aging and dull skin.

### 🧬 Collagen Support

Thanks to its vitamin C, it boosts collagen synthesis for elasticity and strength.

### 🌿 Gut-Skin Connection

Its **prebiotic fiber** feeds healthy gut bacteria. Better gut = better skin clarity.

> _Emerging research shows gut health can significantly influence acne and eczema._ — [NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6048199/)

### 🧪 Mineral Power

Contains calcium, potassium, magnesium — key for hydration and skin regeneration.

## How to Use Baobab Powder

Try adding **1 teaspoon daily** to:

- Smoothies
- Yogurt bowls
- Overnight oats
- Coconut water
- Homemade energy bites

### DIY Face Mask

Mix baobab with honey and turmeric for a natural skin-brightening treatment.

```txt
1 tsp baobab + 1 tsp raw honey + 1/4 tsp turmeric → apply 10 mins, rinse.
```
