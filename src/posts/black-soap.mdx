# 🧼 How African Black Soap is Made

**African Black Soap** (_Ose Dudu_ in Nigeria, _Alata Samina_ in Ghana) is a traditional handmade soap revered for its healing and clarifying effects on skin and scalp.

With its roots in **West African culture**, it’s passed from generation to generation — still made today by **women-led cooperatives** using techniques over a century old.

---

## 🌿 Traditional Ingredients

The natural formula includes:

- 🍌 **Dried plantain skins** and **cocoa pods** — roasted to ash to create alkali
- 🧴 **Palm kernel oil**, **coconut oil**, and **shea butter** — for deep moisture
- 🍯 Sometimes enriched with **honey**, **neem**, or **essential oils**

> 🔗 Learn more: [Shea Institute](https://www.sheilainstitute.org/black-soap-process)

These ingredients are **sun-dried**, roasted, and blended by hand — forming a rich, nutrient-packed paste that is sun-cured into soap bars.
