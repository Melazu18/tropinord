## Baobab: The Ancient Superfruit

Baobab fruit has been cherished across Africa for centuries. Known as the _Tree of Life_, its fruit is rich in antioxidants, vitamin C, and minerals.

> "Baobab powder contains 10 times more vitamin C than oranges."

## Skin-Loving Nutrients

- **Vitamin C** boosts collagen production
- **Calcium & Magnesium** support skin cell regeneration
- **Prebiotic fiber** aids digestion, reflecting in skin clarity

## How to Use

Sprinkle 1 tsp into smoothies, oatmeal, or yogurt.  
Or try it in DIY face masks with honey and turmeric.
